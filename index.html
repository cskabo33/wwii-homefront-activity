The Home Front

World War II · 1941–1945

✦

During World War II, the war was not only fought on the battlefield. Every American family faced new challenges at home — rationing, food shortages, and the call to sacrifice for the war effort. Complete both activities below using your assigned family scenario.

A Note Before You Begin
The five family scenarios in these activities are based on real groups of Americans who lived through World War II. Some scenarios reflect serious injustices — including racial segregation, forced relocation, and unequal access to resources. These differences are intentional and historically accurate. As you work through the activities, pay attention to how your family’s experience compares to others. The home front effort was not equally shared by all Americans, and that is part of the history worth understanding.
